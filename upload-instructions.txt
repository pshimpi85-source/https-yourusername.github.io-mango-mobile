To use your real Mango Mobile photos on the website:

1. Place your image files in the images folder.
2. Use these names:
   - hero-phone.jpg or hero-phone.png
   - front-view.jpg or front-view.png
   - display.jpg or display.png
   - camera.jpg or camera.png
3. The website will use them automatically if the file names match.

If you want, you can also rename the files and update the image paths in index.html.
